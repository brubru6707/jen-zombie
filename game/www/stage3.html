Stage 3 — countdown + world swap

A 30 s countdown. The next world is fetched from this same origin during the countdown, and its geometry is built while the clock runs, so the swap at zero costs no frames. If it is late the clock does not wait — it shows INCOMING and swaps the moment it lands. Canned worlds only; the model is not wired yet.
No headset and no walking: the handheld's stick drives. Push to walk, left and right to turn. Everything else — mobs, dialogue, the snapshot, the lamps — behaves exactly as it does in AR.
